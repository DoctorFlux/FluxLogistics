# FluxLogistics

The first Auction House sale starts a 60-second window. When it ends, chat shows one line per item sold, such as **Sold 20× Linen Cloth · ~3g 40s · ~17s ea**. The next sale starts a fresh window. The per-item amount is a weighted average when unit prices differ. Visit the Auction House to cache listings for notices away from the auctioneer. A `~` marks an estimate after the standard 5% cut or a rounded average. When a mailbox invoice matches, the same summary updates with the actual payout, including any returned deposit.

Open a profession to use Flowcraft. On Forever, choose a recipe in the compact Blizzard list and its plan appears to the right. Profit estimates deduct the standard 5% Auction House cut and show signed coin amounts and profit as a percentage of material cost. A cooling recipe still shows its materials.

On Forever, open Blizzard's **Filter** menu for the **From my bags**, **Best picks**, and **Sell or craft** shortlists. Right-click a native recipe to give it a group or edit its note. **Custom groups** appears in Filter when a grouped recipe is available. Classic has these recipe actions in its existing list and search controls.

Material icons show the required count. The output icon shows the whole plan's output, including the range for variable yields. The output card shows total and per-item crafting costs using guaranteed output, and the total and per-item AH sell estimate before fees. The AH row shows the sale count if it differs from the output. Material cards show the planned total and the cost per unit. Hover for allocations, make-versus-buy comparisons and known vendor prices. Click a card for sourcing actions. Drag to pan, use Ctrl+wheel to zoom, or right-click **Fit** to reset. Drag the lower-right grip to resize the profession window.

Right-click **To Craft** to choose between minimizing total material value and minimizing new gold spent. The same menu can select Auctionator, TradeSkillMaster, or automatic pricing. The choices apply to new previews. Hover the estimate for its objective, additional spending, and available market source information.

- **Queue** accepts the entered amount. **Queue All** uses the largest eligible batch from unreserved carried materials, including craftable intermediates. **Create** queues the whole request and acts on its first ready step. **Create All** queues the largest eligible batch and acts on its first step. Unfilled requirements stay queued. **To Craft**, **Profit** and cooldown information appear above these controls.
- In Forever, **Process** and the FluxUI **Craft** reminder start a verified batch of an eligible ordinary recipe. After the batch settles, press **Process** for the next ready step. Recipes that need Blizzard's form open it with the queued amount filled in. Use Blizzard's **Create** when the ready message appears. The flowchart can sit beside the native form. **Native UI** expands the form while keeping its prepared batch.
- If the next ready step needs another profession, **Process** opens it and focuses the queued step. The FluxUI reminder can open the required profession too. Once it is ready, press **Process** to craft. Eligible stopped jobs are rebuilt automatically from carried materials. **Open profession** opens the required skill first if needed.
- The compact queue sits below the create buttons. Compatible repeat requests share a row, and completed steps leave the active list. **Process** advances the selected job or finds a ready step in the open profession. On Forever, a verified interruption keeps completed crafts and returns the remaining amount to **Process**. Use **X** to remove a row or **Clear** to clear the queue. An already submitted action keeps its result record while it settles. Right-click a job to reorder it or manage its remaining work.
- FluxUI protects any item used by an unfinished queued craft from being marked, sold or destroyed as junk. Protection includes paused crafts and their intermediates. Your existing junk preferences apply again when the queue no longer needs that item.
- Open a merchant to automatically buy queued reagents available there within the approved quantity and price limits. A cheaper merchant offer can also fill an eligible auction requirement. The existing FluxUI **Restock** buttons use the same shortages, including withdrawals from available character-bank stock. Bag receipts reduce the temporary target. Pause a job to defer its restock.
- Click **Shopping List** below the queue to see outstanding auction materials. It also opens beside the Auction House when queued materials are needed. For the selected recipe, click the output card and choose **Shop this craft**. Click **Queue plan** on a material to accept the plan and prepare its offer. Each row shows **Find offer** or **Buy ×N**. The FluxUI **Restock** tab can buy the same prepared offer. On legacy clients each press buys one eligible listing. The list's **Auctionator** button searches all outstanding amounts together. The profession header's **Auctionator** button searches the recipe's output and direct materials. Pending deliveries reduce the list quantities. After you collect the materials, FluxUI's restock follow-up button can process the next eligible craft.
- With the Auction House open, **Check prices** compares available listings for materials and their craftable intermediates. The plan can make part of an intermediate from cheaper materials and buy the remainder. Queue the new estimate to approve its quantities and copper limits. Shopping rows show item icons, the quote or budget in coins, and any incoming quantity. The main Shopping List button updates after each confirmed purchase. The same temporary materials are visible in FluxUI's **Restock** tab until the items reach your bags. Classic buys one listed stack per press. A price increase outside the approved budget requires a new check.
- With both windows open, **Shopping List** docks beneath the Auction House tabs when the screen has enough room. **Find offer** waits for an active Auctionator search to finish. If a cached estimate is too low, **Live offer** shows the current price for a separate **Buy** click. The price shown applies to one purchase. After confirmation, the same footer button prepares the next stack or material. The accepted plan remains intact.
- With automatic sourcing, Shopping compares the auction price for the needed reagent quantity with the full cost of the vendor packs it would replace. It shows **Buy** when an eligible auction is cheaper. Otherwise it keeps the shortage for the vendor and shows the vendor cost. Paid auction deliveries count toward the same requirement, so merchant restock waits for them.
- **Review** is available when a craft result still needs confirmation. When the remaining work can be rebuilt from bags, the previous job stays in history. **Retry** returns a verified older blocked attempt to the queue. Collect paid auction deliveries before using **Edit remaining**.
- **Native UI** returns to Blizzard's presentation. Use **Flowcraft** on Forever or `/fluxlogistics show` on Classic to return.
- Use `/fluxlogistics status` to inspect provider readiness, the current plan, the last craft and the last acquisition. New action records include the addon and client builds that issued them.
- **Restock plans** in FluxUI's bank, vendor and Auction House surfaces shows the shared target, ready stock, accepted coverage and uncovered demand. Unlock passive planning by selecting an activity mode and enabling managed restock in FluxUI.

FluxLogistics requires `_FluxUI`. FluxUI supplies bags, bank and mail through its built-in inventory, and switches to Syndicator when the installed provider is ready. Auctionator is the preferred market provider, with support for TradeSkillMaster prices.

## Builds and validation

Run `powershell -NoProfile -File Tools/Package.ps1 -Target all` to build Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Anniversary and Classic Era archives under `dist`. Retail's archive supplies the sale notices on its own. Install the other clients' archives alongside a FluxUI build with the version 1 restock bridge. See [integration and rollback](docs/INTEGRATION.md), the [validation matrix](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_MATRIX.md) and [client source evidence](docs/CLIENT_EVIDENCE.md).

Desktop fixtures cover planner behavior, native-action contracts through stubs, workbench state and both addons' restock integration. The client evidence ledger records each outstanding native check. The [product backlog](docs/BACKLOG.md) retains the account logistics milestones.
