# FluxUI integration and rollback

## Ownership

FluxLogistics observes bags, bank and mail via `Flux.Inventory`. FluxUI uses its built-in backend until a compatible loaded Syndicator becomes ready, then publishes through the same facade. Readiness and reliability are required before a count is accepted. The Catalog uses FluxUI profession observations, with live recipe/session qualification in the client adapter. Market adapters use Auctionator's public API or the selected TSM expression. A known output variant requires a matching market record before displaying an estimated sale result. Cached price age and provenance remain estimates.

The planner's revision identifies the recipe/catalog snapshot, observed inventory, selected quotes, qualified offers, quantity and policy. A preview carries the goal, graph, ordered tasks, allocations, acquisition requirements and cost summary. Queue acceptance validates its revision and exact planned action data before saving. Estimates travel with the accepted plan. An explicit edit creates a replacement for remaining work and preserves the previous job's action history.

Local stock targets use unallocated guaranteed output from accepted, pending crafts. The covered chart shows the requested target, its carried units and its commitments. Future output contributes to planning coverage while carried receipts continue to come from the inventory provider.

## Version 1 owner bridge

`Flux.RestockPolicy` belongs to FluxUI. FluxUI supplies effective targets, tiers, scope, activity modes, membership, thresholds, flags and count units. `GetDemands({manual = true})` returns the effective rows for manual inspection. Passive orders require the owner's mode and managed setting. Disabled presets retain their state. Ready stock derives from the destination observations. Accepted coverage appears separately.

- `RegisterPlanner` takes `Request(demand, action, context)`, `GetCoverage(demand, ownerQuantity, observedItemID)`, `GetRequirements()`, `GetPlanStatus(demand)`, `IsWorkflowBusy()` and optionally `GetShoppingRequirements()` and `OpenAuctionShopping(parent, entry)`. The optional `observedItemID` identifies the listing item represented in the owner's pending count, including when the preferred item for a tier changes. The shopping projection supplies live amounts for the Auction House submenu. `GetRequirements()` retains the accepted requirements used for reservations. A coverage entry has a stable job identity and outstanding quantity. A confirmed bag receipt reduces the prospective quantity as the owner's physical observation increases.
- The optional `GetCraftMaterialIDs()` returns a read-only set keyed by the numeric item IDs of materials needed by accepted, unfinished craft steps. FluxUI uses it for junk classification, manual junk marking and sell checks. The hold applies to every copy of the item because the junk list is keyed by item ID. It leaves the user's saved junk preferences intact.
- `GetAcquisitionRequirements("bank" or "vendor")` projects temporary materials onto the existing Restock buttons. `AcquireRequirement(surface, selection)` validates the specific job, plan revision, item and current quantity. The bank may use eligible character-bank stock of the same material while respecting other jobs' reservations and any paid auction receipt. Pending actions remain visible until bag confirmation. Manual bag gains also reduce idle shortages. The rows do not become saved presets.
- `GetAcquisitions(itemID, actorKey)` exposes qualified live carried-to-bags vendor and bank routes with location, unit, bundle, price and inventory evidence. The planner subtracts already accepted offers from remaining availability.
- `GetVendorPriceEstimate(itemID)` exposes a typed gold bundle price from current-character observations or the owner catalog. An automatic purchase needs a matching live offer, the accepted quantity and price limits, current funds and an opted-in queued job. A nonexecutable estimate can match a different live pack within its original limits. A live accepted quote retains its exact vendor terms. The merchant Shift bypass defers automatic purchasing. Distinct materials from one craft order are sent in the same merchant pass. Unlimited stock can be split into bounded native-size purchases against one receipt. Pending quantities clear after carried confirmation.
- `ExecuteAcquisition(offer, quantity, validate)` is the owner action path. The accepted offer and remaining job are checked against live terms and native context. Vendor acquisitions distinguish allocated materials still needed from the physical whole-bundle quantity authorized and charged. A newly selected provider can resume an unissued vendor purchase after a reliable carried count. An issued purchase interrupted by a provider switch requires review.
- A lower-priced live merchant offer may fulfill a queued auction requirement through manual Restock or enabled automatic purchase once inbox coverage is verified and the job has no paid incoming units for that requirement. The exact quantity and both accepted spending limits apply across merchant and auction spending. Explicit Buy choices in new plans lock the auction route. An older plan without an approved price needs a refreshed plan before it can buy.
- `GetShoppingRequirements("auction")` projects accepted queue needs, including materials for tasks waiting on a prerequisite craft. The **Shopping List** opens from the crafting queue and appears beside the Auction House for outstanding auction materials. FluxUI's **Restock plans → Shopping** opens the same list at the selected job requirement. The list groups identical materials and applies pending deliveries to its quantity column. **Shop this craft** opens the current draft and its row buttons can accept it and prepare a quote. The list's **Auctionator** button searches all outstanding purchase quantities, including vendor-listed reagents, for price comparison. The profession header's **Auctionator** searches the output and all reachable ingredients. The selected craft route carries its actual quantities, and alternative ingredients are searched for price only. A deliberate **Find offer** click can prepare a quote while FluxUI Restock is active. The row and Restock buttons both validate that quote against the same job, quantity and price limits. One press submits one authorized order or legacy listing.
- An explicit live review can quote an ordinary cached-price requirement against current listings. The temporary quote leaves the plan revision, original estimate and explicit vendor or restock limits intact. A buy click carries the shown quantity and total. The owner and companion reject a later increase or a changed quantity before dispatch. Confirmed spending remains attached to the original requirement and prepares the next quote through the same controls.
- FluxUI's existing restock follow-up button can process a queued recipe after an auction purchase when its carried materials and reservations allow the next craft. It rechecks the displayed job and batch on each press. Opening another profession can require a separate press before native submission. Merchant auto-buy runs on the first safe frame after FluxUI's live inventory index becomes ready.
- FluxUI's optional `ScanLogisticsBooks` callback supplies bounded current auction observations for the recipe's reachable inputs. A larger recipe scan requests consecutive batches of up to 24 items. Each book identifies commodity tiers or indivisible listings and records whether the scan completed. The planner shares those observed quantities across its candidate route. It separates the total budget from the highest approved price and keeps exact listing sizes and limits for stacked offers. A confirmed purchase reduces the queue shortage and invalidates the earlier price observation for that item. A restock request from the Auction House checks current depth and revalidates the stock target before accepting work.
- Direct replenishment for a FluxUI stock target checks the owner's latest destination stock and coverage before a bank move or purchase. The queue assigns remaining room in job order. The same job uses its accepted bank and vendor routes ahead of the Auction House, within the approved quantities and bundle terms. Pending and paid receipts retain their coverage. One-time supply orders retain their accepted quantities.
- Auction purchases check the accepted unit cap, whole quantity limit, remaining budget, current offer, money and context before submission. Auctionator estimates can contain fractional copper. Plan estimates retain that precision, while purchase authorization rounds the unit and total limits down to whole copper. A craft plan can authorize a single auction purchase whose total beats buying a whole vendor bundle. Ordinary Restock applies its own vendor unit-price comparison. With reliable carried counts and a readable FluxUI pending-purchase ledger, a manual Buy can use known incoming coverage before the mailbox is opened. Only observed physical mail counts as collectable. Older providers without the observed-count interface still require a verified inbox. Confirmed purchases continue to count as incoming after their job is cancelled and can supply other jobs. Carried observations determine when a craft can use those materials.

Normal offers manual planning. Open world inherits common targets. Raid/dungeon inherits both. The owner checks eligible passive rules in batches of up to 12, so an unsupported rule cannot continuously block later ones. For each activity, it resolves tier overlap and stable coverage IDs before calculating the uncovered quantity. A bags-plus-bank rule already includes eligible bank stock. A bags-target rule credits a bank withdrawal once it is allocated, then shifts that quantity into ready stock when bags confirm it.

The executable actor is the current character/build. Delivery goals retain a destination identity. Confirmed production in the current character's bags can satisfy a local bags delivery. A job targeting another destination remains awaiting delivery. Account movement is part of the [later milestones](BACKLOG.md).

## Migration and rollback

On a supported client, first load copies the existing `FluxCraftDB` and `FluxCraftCharDB` into `FluxLogisticsDB` and `FluxLogisticsCharDB` if the new roots are absent. The new roots keep the previous catalog, saved UI and queues isolated by character/build. Both legacy roots remain declared for evidence and rollback. Existing pending jobs carry their old route. An interrupted craft across reload needs a matched craft result and a carried-output checkpoint from the same active batch. Older jobs without the checkpoint stay in review until the player removes the unresolved job with its **X**.

Close the game before replacing source or restoring a backup. Prefer restoring the matching original addon source and retaining both SavedVariables roots. The older source reads its legacy roots. Work recorded after migration belongs to the new roots. For FluxUI rollback, restore the scoped owner runtime files from the dated checkpoint and retain the previous settings backup.
